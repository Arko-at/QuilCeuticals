QUILCEUTICALS™️ FACE CREAM

Product Positioning & Marketing Brief

Brand

QuilCeuticals™️

Brand Philosophy

Skincare should serve your skin—not the algorithm.

QuilCeuticals is being developed as a premium dermocosmetic skincare brand combining Italian craftsmanship, natural-origin science, barrier-conscious care, and purposeful simplicity.

The brand is not built around excessive routines, viral ingredients, or encouraging consumers to continually add more products. The objective is sophisticated, purposeful skincare in which every product has a defined role.

Brand expression: Just Your Skin™️

---

1. Product Concept

The QuilCeuticals Face Cream is a premium daily facial moisturizer designed around hydration, barrier support, skin comfort, and a healthy luminous appearance.

Rather than positioning the cream around one fashionable hero ingredient, the formulation brings together complementary ingredients that address several fundamental requirements of healthy-looking skin.

Key Formula Story

Niacinamide — targeted at 4%
Supports the appearance of an even complexion and healthy skin barrier.

Ectoin
A multifunctional ingredient selected for skin protection, hydration and resilience.

Bifida Ferment Lysate
Supports the brand's microbiome-conscious and barrier-focused formulation philosophy.

Hyaluronic Acid
Provides hydration and supports a plump, conditioned skin appearance.

Ceramides
Selected to support the skin barrier and reduce the simplistic “just moisturize it” approach to facial care.

Squalane
Provides emollience and supports softness without defining the product as a conventional heavy cream.

Pearl Extract
Adds to the premium formulation story and luminous-skin positioning.

---

2. Core Positioning

A sophisticated daily face cream that supports the skin rather than overwhelming it.

The marketing should communicate:

Hydrate. Support. Protect. Simplify.

The product should not be presented as another moisturizer competing primarily on fragrance, packaging, or a single trending ingredient.

Its differentiation comes from the complete formulation philosophy.

---

3. What Makes It Different

Purposeful formulation

Every major ingredient should have a reason for being present. The story is not “more ingredients = better skincare.”

Barrier-conscious

The product recognizes the skin barrier as fundamental to healthy-looking skin.

Microbiome-conscious formulation

Bifida Ferment Lysate provides an important connection to QuilCeuticals' broader PRE + POST BIOTIC / Quilbiotics™️ product architecture where substantiated and legally appropriate.

No dependence on fragrance

The sensory experience should come from the formulation itself rather than using perfume as the principal luxury signal.

Premium Italian positioning

The product is intended to carry a Made in Italy manufacturing story, subject to final manufacturing and labeling qualification.

Simplicity without being basic

QuilCeuticals should communicate that sophisticated skincare does not require an unnecessarily complicated routine.

---

4. Target Consumer

The core consumer is someone who:

- Wants premium skincare but is tired of excessive routines.
- Reads ingredient labels and wants to understand what they are buying.
- Values skin-barrier health.
- Wants hydration and visible skin quality without chasing every skincare trend.
- Appreciates science but does not want skincare presented like a pharmaceutical textbook.
- Is willing to pay for formulation quality and positioning rather than simply the number of ingredients in a jar.

---

5. Desired Consumer Perception

The consumer should come away thinking:

“This feels sophisticated, intentional and trustworthy. They didn't put ingredients in here just because they are trending.”

The product should feel premium and intelligent, not clinical to the point of sterility and not “natural” to the point of appearing scientifically unsophisticated.

---

6. Messaging Pillars

SKIN FIRST

The skin—not social-media trends—determines the formulation philosophy.

BARRIER CONSCIOUS

Healthy-looking skin begins with supporting its fundamental barrier.

PURPOSEFUL SCIENCE

Ingredients are selected to work within a coherent formulation rather than to create a long marketing list.

ITALIAN CRAFTSMANSHIP

European formulation and manufacturing become part of the premium brand experience.

PURPOSEFUL SIMPLICITY

Consumers should not need ten products to feel that they are taking their skincare seriously.

---

7. Marketing Language Territory

Strong language territories include:

Just Your Skin™️

Skincare should serve your skin—not the algorithm.

Purposeful skincare. Nothing performative.

Barrier-conscious care for skin that needs support, not noise.

Italian craftsmanship. Purposeful science.

Final cosmetic claims must be reviewed against the finished formulation, testing and applicable U.S. regulatory requirements before publication.

---

8. Brand Guardrails

The marketing should not:

- Position QuilCeuticals as another viral skincare brand.
- Build the entire identity around one ingredient.
- Imply that consumers need a complicated multi-step routine.
- Use exaggerated medical or drug-like claims.
- Promise treatment or cure of skin disease.
- Overuse words such as “miracle,” “revolutionary,” or “instant transformation.”
- Make the brand appear inexpensive or mass-market.
- Allow influencer trends to dictate the scientific identity of the product.

---

9. Commercial Positioning

The Face Cream is intended for the premium skincare category, with a target retail range previously considered around $78–$114.

The objective is not to compete on lowest price.

The consumer should understand why the formulation, manufacturing story, ingredient architecture and brand philosophy justify premium positioning.

---

10. Strategic Summary for Marketing Team

QuilCeuticals Face Cream is not selling another step in a skincare routine. It is selling a more disciplined philosophy of skincare: support the skin barrier, provide purposeful hydration, use sophisticated ingredients intelligently, and remove unnecessary noise.

That philosophy should remain recognizable across every advertisement, website page, social post, campaign and influencer partnership.




QUILCEUTICALS™️ BODY LOTION

Product Positioning & Marketing Brief

Brand

QuilCeuticals™️

Central Brand Idea

Just Your Skin™️

QuilCeuticals approaches body skincare with the same seriousness normally reserved for facial skincare.

The body lotion should therefore not be marketed simply as something that smells pleasant and temporarily makes dry skin feel soft.

The strategic opportunity is to elevate everyday body care into premium, barrier-conscious skincare.

---

1. Product Concept

A sophisticated daily body lotion designed to moisturize, condition and support healthy-looking skin while fitting into QuilCeuticals' philosophy of purposeful skincare.

Important Formula Direction

Niacinamide — targeted at approximately 3%

A meaningful component of the body-care positioning, supporting skin-barrier and even-looking-skin messaging within permitted cosmetic claims.

Additional formulation directions discussed include:

N-Acetyl Glucosamine (NAG)
Selected as a complementary ingredient within the skin-conditioning and appearance-focused formulation strategy.

Licorice-derived ingredient
Considered for the formulation to support the appearance-focused complexion story.

The final marketing team must work from the final approved INCI/formula before publishing ingredient-specific claims.

---

2. Market Problem

Body lotion is frequently marketed around:

- fragrance,
- basic moisturization,
- oversized value packaging,
- seasonal dryness,
- or cosmetic indulgence.

QuilCeuticals should take a different position.

Why should consumers who carefully select sophisticated ingredients for their faces accept generic formulations for the majority of their skin?

That question provides a powerful strategic foundation for the product.

---

3. Core Positioning

FACE-LEVEL THINKING FOR BODY SKIN.

The lotion should communicate that the skin below the neck deserves intentional formulation too.

This does not mean literally calling it a facial product for the body. It means bringing the philosophy of sophisticated skincare into daily body care.

---

4. Differentiation

Body skincare rather than “just lotion”

The marketing distinction is important.

QuilCeuticals should occupy skincare territory, not commodity moisturizer territory.

Barrier-conscious

Hydration is important, but the brand story extends beyond temporary softness.

Purposeful active selection

Niacinamide and the broader ingredient strategy allow the lotion to tell a more sophisticated story than conventional fragrance-led body lotion.

No fragrance-led identity

The brand should not depend upon a perfume profile to make the lotion feel premium.

Italian craftsmanship

The intended Made in Italy positioning gives the product an additional premium manufacturing and formulation narrative, subject to final qualification.

---

5. Target Consumer

The ideal consumer:

- Invests in facial skincare but has historically used ordinary body lotion.
- Wants the rest of their skin treated with similar consideration.
- Values smooth, hydrated, healthy-looking skin.
- Prefers sophisticated formulations to heavily fragranced body products.
- Wants premium skincare without an unnecessarily complicated routine.
- Is interested in ingredient quality and formulation philosophy.

---

6. Marketing Opportunity

The strategic story is larger than:

“Our lotion moisturizes.”

The stronger narrative is:

Your body is skin too.

Consumers have been conditioned to treat facial skincare as science and body skincare as basic maintenance.

QuilCeuticals can challenge that distinction.

---

7. Messaging Pillars

YOUR BODY IS SKIN

Body care deserves thoughtful formulation.

BEYOND MOISTURIZATION

The product should be understood as daily skincare, not merely relief from dryness.

PURPOSEFUL ACTIVES

Ingredients should be communicated based on their role in the finished formulation.

BARRIER-CONSCIOUS CARE

Support the skin rather than repeatedly stripping and covering it.

ITALIAN CRAFTSMANSHIP

Premium formulation and manufacturing should reinforce the brand's elevated positioning.

---

8. Potential Messaging Territory

Just Your Skin™️

Your body is skin too.

Body care, reconsidered as skincare.

More than moisture. Purposeful care for the skin you're in.

Italian craftsmanship. Barrier-conscious care.

Claims must be substantiated against the final formulation and appropriate testing before commercial use.

---

Do not reduce this product to:

“luxurious lotion.”

Avoid making fragrance the center of communication.

Avoid presenting it as a cheap high-volume moisturizer.

Avoid exaggerated pigmentation, medical, eczema, healing, repair, or therapeutic claims unless the finished product and regulatory pathway specifically support them.

Avoid creating dozens of benefits simply because individual raw materials have supplier literature associated with them.

Market the finished product—not a collection of ingredient studies.

---

10. Commercial Positioning

Previously considered retail positioning:

Approximately $48–$65 for 250 mL.

At this price level, the marketing must communicate why this is fundamentally different from commodity body lotion.

Packaging, photography, language, website design and advertising should consistently communicate premium dermocosmetic body skincare.

---

11. Strategic Summary for Marketing Team

QuilCeuticals Body Lotion exists to challenge the idea that sophisticated skincare stops at the face.

The marketing opportunity is to move the consumer mentally from:

“I need body lotion.”

to:

“I should care for all of my skin with the same intention.”

That is the territory QuilCeuticals should own.

Brand

Brand Philosophy

---

1. Product Concept

The QuilCeuticals Cleanser is intended as a premium cleansing product built around an important principle:

Cleansing should remove what the skin doesn't need without treating the skin itself as the problem.

Cleansing is the beginning of a skincare routine. An aggressive first step can undermine everything that follows.

QuilCeuticals therefore approaches cleansing as part of barrier-conscious skincare, rather than simply producing foam.

---

2. Consumer Problem

Consumers often associate:

more foam = cleaner skin

or

tightness = deep cleansing.

That is not the positioning QuilCeuticals should reinforce.

The desired consumer experience is effective cleansing with a formulation philosophy that respects the skin.

The prototype has demonstrated foaming capability, so the product can provide the sensory reassurance many consumers expect without making aggressive cleansing the brand identity.

---

3. Core Positioning

CLEAN SKIN WITHOUT THE STRIPPED-SKIN PHILOSOPHY.

The cleanser should establish QuilCeuticals' entire skincare philosophy from the first step.

The objective is not merely to remove surface impurities.

It is to begin the skincare routine without unnecessarily compromising the skin experience the subsequent products are designed to support.

---

4. Differentiation

Barrier-conscious cleansing

Cleansing is treated as skincare rather than preparation for skincare.

Purposeful simplicity

Consumers do not need aggressive sensory signals to know that cleansing occurred.

Skin-respecting philosophy

The formulation should reinforce the broader QuilCeuticals belief that skin does not need to be constantly attacked, stripped and corrected.

No dependence on fragrance

The product experience should come from formulation performance rather than perfume.

Italian craftsmanship

The intended Made in Italy story supports the premium European skincare positioning, subject to the final manufacturing arrangement and labeling qualification.

---

5. Target Consumer

The cleanser is intended for consumers who:

- Want effective daily cleansing.
- Dislike an excessively stripped or tight post-cleanse feeling.
- Are becoming more conscious of their skin barrier.
- Use premium skincare and understand that cleansing affects the rest of the routine.
- Prefer thoughtful formulation over gimmicks.
- Want a simple routine without sacrificing sophistication.

---

6. Strategic Role Within the QuilCeuticals System

The cleanser is especially important because it introduces the consumer to the QuilCeuticals philosophy.

STEP 1 — CLEANSE

Remove what doesn't belong.

STEP 2 — SUPPORT

Use purposeful skincare to support hydration and skin condition.

STEP 3 — MAINTAIN

Encourage consistency rather than constantly changing routines according to trends.

This gives marketing a simple system without forcing consumers into an excessive number of steps.

---

7. Messaging Pillars

CLEANSING IS SKINCARE

The first step should respect what the later steps are trying to support.

CLEAN, NOT PUNISHED

Do not equate harshness with efficacy.

BARRIER CONSCIOUS

The skin barrier remains part of the formulation philosophy even at the cleansing stage.

PURPOSEFUL SIMPLICITY

Do what the product needs to do—and do it well.

ITALIAN CRAFTSMANSHIP

Use manufacturing provenance as part of the premium story without allowing “Made in Italy” to become the only reason to buy the product.

---

8. Marketing Language Territory

Just Your Skin™️

Cleanse your skin. Don't fight it.

Cleansing is skincare.

Clean without the stripped-skin philosophy.

The first step should respect every step that follows.

Purposeful cleansing. Barrier-conscious care.

The marketing team should avoid:

- Equating aggressive foam with superior cleansing.

The cleanser is a 250 mL premium skincare product.
